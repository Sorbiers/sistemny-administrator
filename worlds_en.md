# «System Administrator» — bible of the worlds and the style

## 1. Our time: Moscow, 2001–2004

### Background of the era (in passing, never head-on)
- The dollar at about thirty roubles; salaries «in an envelope», in dollars.
- Mobile phones with monochrome screens, per-second tariffs, «flash me» (a missed call as a signal).
- A rented one-room flat on the outskirts, suburban trains, minibuses, the market by the metro.
- Gangster series on television; in the office, Flash cartoons and jokes sent round by email.
- Big events (the theatre siege in 2002, the arrest of the biggest oilman in 2003) are heard only as talk in the accounts office.

### Technology (what the hero lives by)
- A Windows 2000 server under the chief accountant's desk, workstations on Windows 98.
- «1C» accounting, version 7; the database crashes once a week.
- Email on the firm's own server, unencrypted ICQ, remote desktop control — all of it legitimately part of an admin's job.
- Internet: first a modem, then a leased line, traffic paid by the megabyte.
- «Bank-client» software with a key floppy disk kept in a safe three metres from Igor.
- Parts bought at the radio market; the internet is too thin for his «night» research, so — the library and the second-hand book stalls.

The theft is described in an everyday way, without technical detail: what matters is not the scheme but that for Igor it is a routine operation.

### Furniture Paradise
Forty people. Created in the nineties on the premises and machines of a privatised factory, hence the name. The furniture didn't sell; wooden windows and panelled doors do well. The office — two floors on the site of the former Moscow Theatre Furniture Factory: an entrance with a guard, neighbouring tenants, a theatre seat in the smoking corner, linoleum, a suspended ceiling, a kitchen with a notice «Whoever takes other people's sugar — have a conscience». The workshop is outside Moscow. The accounts office is the warmest room; it holds the safe, the server, the ficus and Igor.

### Meridian Holding
Six hundred people, building-materials trade, a business centre with a glass lift and passes. A mission statement on the wall, a corporate newspaper, a security service, an air-conditioned server room. Everything the same as at Furniture Paradise, but with presentations.

### Office types and situations (a reserve for chapters)
Payday; a birthday with a cake and a collection of «a hundred roubles each»; the office party; the tax inspection; a broken printer as a natural disaster; «everything's gone»; the smoking corner; the war over the air conditioner; a dismissal everyone knows about except the person dismissed; New Year «secret Santa»; team building with ropes.

## 2. The dream world

### Rules
1. **Log in.** Igor falls asleep here and wakes there — in the same place and at the same moment where he «logged out». Time there runs only while he is there.
2. **Acceleration.** A night holds more and more: a day → a month → a year → a decade.
3. **Body.** Doesn't age. Wounds stay until the next entry. Death there means waking here; the next night he enters whole («reboot»).
4. **Transfer.** Only knowledge goes there. Only skills, calluses, a tan and the habit of giving orders come back.
5. **Ambiguity.** No rule proves the world is real. The sacred words coincide with his vocabulary — either a trace of a dead civilisation, or a dream made out of his own head. The book doesn't choose.
6. **Price.** The longer he rules, the stronger the pull to sleep. By the end — sleeping pills, days off, sleep in the server room.

### The hero's three guesses
| Stage | What he thinks | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Part I | The past, the ancient Slavs | Log huts, skins, a language that sounds old |
| Part II | A parallel world | No known history; sabre-toothed wolves (first in chapter 1) and other impossible animals; a sea where there can be none |
| Part IV | The far future, Moscow | Tunnels with tiles and the letter «M»; he recognises the station |

The reader is given the clues earlier than the hero.

### What happened to the old world
A nuclear war begun by an accidental launch. After it — nuclear winter, volcanic eruptions, a shift of the landscape, an ice age. Then life was reborn. The book states this as a fact, in a sentence or two: without detail, without scientific explanation and without an account of how everything recovered.

Nothing of the old civilisation is left on the surface. Only what is deep underground survives, and a few words in the language.

### Geography (future Moscow)
| There | What it is |
|---|---|
| Vykh | Vykhino — the only part of Moscow that did not go under the water. The first settlement, later the capital |
| The Big Water | A sea to the west, where the rest of Moscow was |
| The river | Nameless, flows into the sea. On its bank Igor wakes on the first night |
| The Lyubers | Enemy neighbours to the east (Lyubertsy) |
| The forest | Unbroken, with no trace of people. Sabre-toothed wolves and other animals that never existed before |
| The lower passages | Buried metro tunnels deep underground — the only surviving trace. Found in Part IV |

### Sacred words (the faith before Igor came)
- **Login** — the way in, the gate, the beginning.
- **Password** — an oath, a secret word.
- **Admin** — the supreme deity, «the one to whom everything is open».
- **The Net** — the world, fate, the connection of everything with everything.
- **Virus** — plague.
- **Zeroing** — the end of the old world.
- **Reboot** — resurrection.

**What has been shown in chapters 1–4.** The body there is separate: no asthma, no short sight; wounds remain from night to night, and writing in pen on his palm doesn't get through — only what is in his head. One night there lasts about a day. Igor gathers knowledge by day (the internet is useless; the accounts office and the district library help) and learns it by heart.

**The first breach of the rule (chapter 4).** Bor lets the Guest treat his son against Khran's ban: «It holds. But Mal isn't holding.» Mal lives; Bor gives Igor shoes — his first possession there. Veda starts washing her hands. Khran notes that the guest still hasn't said the Password.

**Chapter 5: the deadline and the tablet.** About twenty days and nights have passed (still one for one). Igor has made soap, made fire, become Veda's helper. He sees the sea for the first time and doubts that this is Ancient Rus. He teaches Yasya letters; Khran rubs them out and declares: the guest stays until the ice. When the Big Water freezes and the Lyubers come, Igor must say the Password or leave. On Khran's tablet are two rows of carved signs: the top one — «ЛОГИН» (LOGIN) in block capitals, the bottom one (six or seven signs) covered by Khran's palm.

**The Password (chapter 6).** It is «qwerty» — «Kverti», sacred syllables without meaning. The rules of the rite: three attempts («after the third the gate doesn't open») and the Password is not said aloud — a secret rule known only to Khran. Igor whispers into Khran's ear: «password» — no, «123456» — no, «qwerty» — yes. The firm's password notebook and the «slingshot» sign (Y) on the tablet helped.

**New status: User.** On the fortieth day, when the Big Water froze, Igor «came in»: he has a share and lives in Veda's house by the fire. Khran warns: «Now the Password is with two. Two is a lot.» Igor thinks a password known to two must be changed. Next morning, three smokes in the east: the Lyubers will come over the ice in about five days. Bor asks: «Do you know about this?»

**Chapter 7: «while the smoke stands».** Every winter the Lyubers come over the ice and take a third of the stores and two or three girls («that's not a raid, that's a payroll»); last winter they took Lada, Zhila's daughter. Yasya is not betrothed because she might be taken. Igor proposes not going out into the open: an ice slope before the palisade, a strip of thin ice on the sea with one way through at a marker, stones on the wall, a place for everyone. Khran gives him power for the time of danger: «While the smoke stands — the word is his. When the smoke goes — the word goes back.» Igor draws a plan with names on birchbark; only Yasya can read it, and she becomes his messenger. Yasya asks: «And will you give it back?» On the fifth morning there are not fifty Lyubers but about a hundred.

**Chapter 8: the raid and the first death.** Rudyi (chief of the Lyubers, in a red fur hat) demands the third; at Igor's word Bor answers: «No third!» Half the Lyubers go through the ice, the other half come under the stones at the marker. What Igor didn't provide for: burning arrows on the reed roofs and a stake hacked through beside the gate. He didn't write himself a place on the birchbark. A Lyuber goes for Yasya; Igor ends up in the way and takes a spear under the ribs. Dying, he hears his asthmatic whistle and sees the Lyubers leaving across the ice. He wakes under the grandmother's ceiling.

**The rule of death (adopted, chapter 9).** An ordinary exit pauses that world, but death does not: while Igor is awake here, time goes on there. The next night he enters whole where his body lies.

**Chapter 9: Reboot.** Igor comes to after a night and a day on a funeral platform above an ice hole, while Khran reads the funeral words. The wound has gone; the T-shirt still has a hole; on his chest is a birchbark with Yasya's inscription «ИГОР» (she didn't know the soft sign). Khran is the first to find the word: «Reboot» — and declares that he didn't say Login, he himself is Login, «a Way In that walks». From that day he is called Login; only Yasya calls him Igor. The outcome of the raid: the third and the girls weren't handed over; the Lyubers lost more than forty; in Vykh three died — old Tit, Nega (Zhila's sister) and fourteen-year-old Vyun, whom Igor himself had placed on the corner. Zhila pulled Rudyi out of the water to trade for Lada. Khran reminds him: the smoke has gone, the word must go back. Igor answers: «The smoke hasn't gone. Until it's over. Then I'll give it back.» Yasya nods — it isn't clear whether in approval or because she has made sure of something.

**Chapter 10: time speeds up.** For the first time nine days pass there in one night. The roofs are plastered with clay, the palisade doubled, and a line «who watches the back» appears on the birchbark. Yasya passes on orders with the words «Login said». Rudyi isn't traded but kept as a hostage, «a guest until the grass» (in Zhila's porch, between the woodpile and the goat); for his life the Lyubers return Lada and ten more women taken in earlier winters. Khran notices how people look at Igor. Rudyi becomes his conversation partner: «It doesn't end. There's nobody to give it to — once you look at who, your hand won't move»; he defends the third in the same words as Tamara Pavlovna. On the ninth night Yasya comes for the soft sign, writes «ИГОРЬ» and stays: «Login is the one who got up off the platform, I'm afraid of him. But you couldn't split a log.»

**Chapter 11: spring, accounting, the wedding, «a parallel world».** A night now holds a month. In spring, the hungry season, Login orders the stores in every house counted and brought into a common store under a bar; food is handed out by Yasya's list. For the first time nobody dies of hunger. The key-peg hangs round Igor's neck («And who has the key?» — Zhila). Khran arranges for a house to be built for Login on the high ground: «They need to know where you are.» The wedding with Yasya: «There was one login — now it's for two. There was one password — now it's for two.» Yasya knows that he «goes away when he sleeps» and that he has a wife there: «The water is big. We won't meet.» Rudyi's people have replaced him with a young chief; he stays in Vykh. From his stories Igor realises: there are no cities, princes, horses or iron, yet they speak Russian — so this is not the past but a parallel world. Conclusion: «Here anything goes.» Rudyi: «Careful. I understood the same thing once.» From here the dream text calls the hero Login (in scenes of power) and Igor (in scenes with Yasya and Rudyi).

**Chapter 12: plague among the Lyubers, the tenth.** Half a year passes in a night (summer and autumn of the first year). The Lyubers have a plague from dirty water («a virus» in Khran's language); six of them come to ask for help. Login goes to them with Veda («They'll cut your throat there.» — «I'll get up») and stops the plague in forty days: boiling, separate houses for the sick, new cesspits, burning the dead, twenty days of quarantine with guards round the settlement. In gratitude they offer him a third; he sets a tenth — into a common store, from Vykh too, the key «with me, until things settle down»; he takes two Lyubers to learn counting. Rudyi: «Taking so that they say thank you.» In the second winter, for the first time, there is no smoke. Igor notices that nobody has cancelled the term of his power — «it just never once ended». Yasya is expecting a child in spring.

**Chapter 13: the first punishment and a son.** A year passes in a night (the second year there). Eight-year-old Mal keeps the accounts of the common store on birchbark; he is three sacks short. Login catches the thief by a footprint in ash: it's Zhila, who has made a second key-peg («I took mine»; he has eight mouths and gets food for six). Khran demands, by custom, that his hand be cut off. Instead Login «changes his rights»: Zhila goes from user to guest until the next ice — fed for eight mouths, but with no word in the ring, not allowed near the store or onto the wall, not to carry a spear. Zhila: «Better the hand.» Khran: «Before, a guest was someone who came. Now — whoever you name.» Bor: «He was the first to come with the ice pick.» Yasya: «Better if you'd beaten him… That was Login talking just now.» In spring Yasya gives birth to a son and names him Svet — Light («born with the light»); Mal writes: «Svet. Login's son.» In autumn Zhila gets his word back but keeps silent; nobody speaks first in the ring any more — they wait for Login.

**Chapter 14: the Net (years 3–10).** In two weeks of holiday, eight winters pass there: a night equals a year, then two. Six more settlements join Vykh of their own accord (famine, plague, the fame of «the one who got up»); the terms are the same for all: a tenth into the common store, their own counter, boiling, the key with Login. Trails and runners link the settlements; the union is called by the sacred word «the Net». Bor has fifty permanent guards. Sixteen-year-old Mal teaches boys to count. Literacy is «by clearance», in three levels: numbers, signs, the right to write in Login's name (Yasya, Mal and two of Khran's men). Khran forges an order in Login's name — an eighth instead of a tenth, «for the House of Words», where the T-shirt with the hole hangs as a relic: «You would have ordered it. I thought of it for you.» Igor introduces a seal — the imprint of the key-peg with six notches: «Whatever doesn't have this isn't me. Even Yasya.» Khran: «And you can't disown yourself either.» Igor and Yasya have two children: Svet (7, big-eared, serious) and Zorya (4, red-haired). Yasya, now thirty, cuts a notch on the doorpost above Igor's head and realises that he doesn't age: «I'll overtake you. You'll stand by this doorpost, and I'll walk on.»

**Chapter 15: the count «from the Way In» and Khran's death (winters 10–19).** Years are counted from the spring when Login got up off the platform («from the Way In, such-and-such a winter»). Mal is head of the count; an annual census: 22 settlements, about four thousand souls. Literacy has five levels of clearance, with promotion by Login's word; Khran's son is stuck on the second. Khran has a big «House of Words» opposite Login's house, where people go for comfort. Rudyi died in the fourteenth winter. The seventeenth and eighteenth winters bring crop failures; the stores empty, and for the first time Login has no answer. Khran explains the famine as the Admin's anger: the words have been handed out to many — and demands that all the birchbarks be burned. Bor's guards find a pit under the House of Words with four hundred sacks that people brought the old man themselves over twelve winters. Login orders the pit opened in front of everyone and says nothing. The crowd leads Khran out; everyone waits for Login's word; Yasya begs: «Tell them.» He stays silent. Khran is drowned in the ice hole; to the end he shields the tablet with his palm. Yasya doesn't speak to her husband until spring. Login puts on the tablet (bottom row — QWERTY) next to the key. The grain grows; people say the old man angered the Admin — Login didn't write that, but «didn't order it rubbed out».

**Chapter 16: the second birchbarks (winters 19–30).** After Khran, the key and the tablet are with Login alone; Mal's people sit in the House of Words. Thirty settlements, seven thousand souls; birchbark works like mail. After a runner carrying the annual count drowns, Login orders a copy made of every birchbark and kept in a store by the House of Words — «in case one gets lost». A year later the whole correspondence of the Net lies there; he goes in «to see how it's stacked» and begins reading every day. Yasya (48 winters, half grey) finds out and asks whether he has read her letters to Zorya; he answers: «I read them.» She: «You never do anything, Igor. It all happens by itself.» Among the copies he finds Bor's letter to the head of the eastern post: «River isn't what he was… But I remember carrying him from the river. Light he was, like a boy. Skinned his heels, cried. When I come — I'll tell you.» There's no treason in it; Bor has three hundred spears. Login puts the birchbark back.

**Chapter 17: Bor goes north (winter 31).** Bor, nearly seventy, comes alone and speaks plainly: «It won't end. Give it back — to Mal, to Svet, to a ring from thirty settlements. If they decide badly — you'll correct it.» He reminds Igor that he remembers him «for his sake»: «You couldn't split a log. You changed Mal's rags all night. That one — where is he?» Login reckons there's nobody to give it to, and that a man with three hundred spears who remembers the god crying is dangerous for that alone. In winter he sends Bor to a distant northern post where «strangers have been seen», and splits his guard three ways under new heads with the seal. Bor understands: «I carried you from the river on my shoulder, upside down. And you're sending me north — the same way.» In spring a birchbark arrives: no strangers seen, Bor died of a chest illness. Mal, his son, silently applies the seal. Igor takes out the shoes Bor gave him in the first winter: they are still two fingers too big. Yasya (over fifty, completely white): «Now nobody remembers you. Only me. And I'm old.»

**Chapter 18: Yasya's death (winters 35–44).** Yasya can no longer climb to the high ground by herself; Igor carries her, and people turn away. Mal died in the 38th winter; Svet keeps the count (41, grey temples — visitors take him for the elder). Zorya is in the east and doesn't write. Forty settlements. In the 44th winter Yasya asks to be carried to the water, onto the clay, and draws «ЯСЯ». Learning that «there» not quite two years have passed: «I took you from her for a whole life. And she didn't even notice.» Her words: «You're not a person. A person is someone who moves on. You stand, and we walk past, and you don't feel sorry for us any more — there are many of us and only one of you.» She makes him promise two things: to find someone who isn't afraid of him and not to touch them, whatever they say; and to tell «the small one» that he was here. She dies at dawn; he tucks her hair behind her ear himself and lays a birchbark «ЯСЯ» on her chest. Then he orders the doorpost with the notches taken out and a smooth one put in. From that day nobody calls him Igor; from here on the dream text calls him «the Admin».

**A dream character:** Zhila — the senior hunter, a sceptic («there's no weight on him»); accepted the rite, but not Igor.

**The Guest status (chapter 3).** Whoever has said Login «stands at the gate» and must say the Password. Igor doesn't know the Password, and the priest Khran declares him a Guest: «come in, but not let in». A guest is not driven out and is fed, but is not listened to, is given nothing of theirs and trusted with nothing of others'.

The meanings of the words are forgotten; the rites remain. At first Igor takes it for coincidence.

### How the locals speak
Short sentences, often with the verb at the end, informal «you» to everyone. No borrowings except the sacred words. The archaic feel comes from simplicity, not from «thee» and «thou». Over the centuries the language grows more complex under Igor's influence — and in the finale an imperial official speaks almost like Eduard Maratovich.

### Chronology
| Real time | There | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Autumn 2001 | Weeks | A stranger. Soap, boiling, the first healing |
| 2002 | Years 1–10 | The raid, the defence, dictatorship «for the duration of the war», the first death, the cult, marriage to Yasya |
| Early 2003 | Years 10–60 | Writing, a calendar, a census, tribute, estates as «access levels». Khran executed, Bor removed. Yasya's death |
| Late 2003 | Years 60–200 | Empire, cities, workshops. A dynasty of counters. The revolt of the younger Yasya. The lower passages |
| Spring 2004 | Years 200–300+ | Offices, archives, boredom. The junior scribe |

### How the dictatorship is established (steps the reader should see)
1. Usefulness: he really does save people.
2. Emergency: power «for the time of trouble».
3. Indispensability: all knowledge runs through him.
4. Accounting: whoever writes everything down owns everything.
5. Removing the witnesses of his weakness.
6. The cult: nobody remembers him as a man any more.
7. The apparatus: the system works without him and for itself.
8. Loneliness: nobody tells him the truth.

No step looks like villainy; each is a reasonable administrative decision.

## 3. Style

### «Our time»
- **Model:** Dovlatov, with the tone of «The Office».
- **Person and tense:** first person, past tense. The narrator is Igor — dry, observant, ironic about himself.
- **Sentence:** short. Paragraphs of two to four sentences. Lots of dialogue.
- **Chapter structure:** an anecdote — set-up, turn, last line. The moral is never spoken.
- **Humour:** deadpan; funny things are reported like reference information.
- **Documents in the text:** emails, ICQ fragments, orders, kitchen notices, Igor's morning «logs».
- **Forbidden:** reflections on power, inner monologues longer than three lines, authorial judgement.

### «Sessions»
- **Model:** Pelevin (parable, irony about the sacred), Limonov's «Podrostok Savenko» (the hero seen from outside, present tense).
- **Person and tense:** third person, present tense. «Login walks to the river.»
- **The hero's name in the text changes** with him: Igor → Login → Admin → Administrator → He. That's how the estrangement shows.
- **Sentence:** longer, denser, more bodily — smells, cold, tiredness. By the end it becomes bureaucratic.
- **The uncanny:** strange things aren't explained and surprise nobody.
- **Forbidden:** fantasy vocabulary, «time-traveller» delight in one's own knowledge, picturesque battles.

### General rules
- The two streams don't comment on each other; the reader, not the hero, sees the rhymes.
- Chapters of roughly equal length (2,000–2,500 words).
- Each day chapter — one office type or ritual; each night chapter — one step towards power.
- The change in Igor is shown through how others speak to him.

## The dream world in chapters 19–26 (briefly)

- **Ch. 19.** He is called the Admin. The first campaign against the northerners; conquest is called «connecting», execution — «disconnecting». In the 48th winter — iron, then wheels and horned draught animals; Vykh is a city of 12 thousand. He walks the lower town unrecognised; asked about him, everyone gives the learned answer «merciful».
- **Ch. 20–21.** Yasya of the eastern trail (from the line of Yasya's sister) tells him to his face that he is «not a god but an owner». He orders that she not be touched, but has her watched; the reports are kept in a store called «the ear». She: «You're kind. That's the worst of it.»
- **Ch. 22.** Svet dies at 76 («I was afraid of you: you'd look — and past»). Yasya's people sit silently on the square; he orders every tenth disconnected; they go round her. «Better if you'd touched me.»
- **Ch. 23.** While quarrying stone they find a tunnel and a station. On an enamel map he reads «…ГАНСКАЯ» and «ВЫХИ…»: this is the future Moscow. The cause of the old world's end is given in a single authorial paragraph. He realises he isn't building but repeating, orders the pit filled in and takes away a shard of enamel.
- **Ch. 24.** In documents he is the Administrator. Decrees go out without him: a cast was taken of the key long ago. In the cellar of the counting house — a second book: the counters take a third. Replacing the head changes nothing.
- **Ch. 25.** In documents — «He». A city of a hundred thousand, offices. At a well a red-haired boy tells him: «There's no such name.»
- **Ch. 26.** In the counting house, between the treasure chest and a tub with a little tree, sits a junior scribe who carries the letters. He isn't afraid. «He» doesn't touch him and gives him advice: «Read — go ahead. Write — never.» The last words of the novel — «Log in».
