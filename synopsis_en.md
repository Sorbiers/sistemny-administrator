# «System Administrator» — synopsis

*The background line of the collapse of civilisation is set in italics.*

## Logline

A quiet sysadmin, Igor Logínov, spends his days reading other people's email at a firm that makes wooden windows — and every night wakes up in a world where he is taken for a messenger of the gods. Three years here and several centuries there turn the «nerd» into the owner of a holding company and an immortal dictator — and by the end it is no longer clear which of his two lives is the dream.

## Title

«System Administrator» plays on two meanings of «system» and «administrator». The modest sysadmin is the Administrator (dictator) of the System (the world). In the office it is an invisible job: the man who fixes printers and knows the passwords. In the dream it is the title of a ruler of an entire world. The book is about how one quietly turns into the other.

## Time and place

Russia, Moscow, the early 2000s — autumn 2001 to spring 2004.

## The hero

**Igor Logínov** — stressed on the «i»: a pun on the English «log in» and the familiar Russian surname Lóginov. A young man of 25, modest, an indecisive introvert who never finished university. Clever. He works as the system administrator of a small firm that makes wooden windows and doors. He came to Moscow from provincial Kirov. He is married to his classmate Sveta — a provincial newcomer like him; no children.

## Plot

The story runs in two parallel streams — «Our time» and «A parallel world in sleep».

**«Our time»** — a pseudo-documentary account of office life at the hero's firm, in the spirit of Sergei Dovlatov and the TV series «The Office». Igor is a modest rank-and-file employee; his desk is in the accounts office, among five women of different ages. But as system administrator he has access to every computer and every information resource in the company — and he doesn't shy away from, indeed rather enjoys, reading his colleagues' correspondence, personal letters included, and the firm's financial documents. In time he gains access to the owner's accounts and deals, and walks off with a major deal and the money behind it. Through an offshore company he secretly becomes the owner of a large holding — and takes a job there as a rank-and-file sysadmin. By the end of the book he catches the CEO stealing, shows his hand and becomes managing director.

**«A parallel world»** — every night, when he lies down to sleep, Igor finds himself in another world; whether in a dream or in reality is unclear. At first he thinks he has landed in the past, later he concludes it is a parallel world, and in the end it turns out to be the far future — life reborn after the collapse of civilisation. Time there runs much faster than in reality. Using what he knows, Igor helps the locals defend themselves against enemies, cope with disease and finally build an advanced civilisation, in which he is the lifelong, ever-young, immortal, «god-sent» ruler: chief, dictator, emperor. Written in the spirit of Victor Pelevin and Eduard Limonov's «Podrostok Savenko». The love story — Yasya, the healer's daughter.

*Between the two streams runs a third, background line — the end of civilisation. By day it is only the noise of the era: radio, television, newspapers, dinner-table talk about the bomb, treaties and false alarms. By night — its consequences: animals that never existed, a sea where no sea can be. A nuclear war begun by an accidental launch and a «guaranteed response», nuclear winter, ice, a shifted earth — and water where Moscow stood, with only Vykhino left above it. This is given as fact, without scientific explanation; the hero pieces the picture together from scraps only when he finds a metro station underground.*

## Idea

As the story goes on, the modest «nerd» Igor, gaining power in the civilisation of his dreams, becomes more and more decisive and ambitious — but also more cynical — in real life.

- **The office as it is.** To show, lightly and with humour, the realities of office life and the personal relationships of people working in large and small offices. To show the typical characters — portraits of office plankton.
- **Growth from inner resources.** To show, through Igor, how a person mobilises his own resources to grow in his career and personal life.
- **The invisible man knows everything.** The modest sysadmin is the best-informed, most influential and most dangerous employee in the company: he knows everything about everyone.
- **The anatomy of dictatorship.** To show how simply and inevitably dictatorships are established, and how the dictator degrades as a person.
- **The price of growth.** To show how personality and family relationships change as career and wealth grow.

## Form

- **Length:** 26 chapters, about 58,000 words.
- **Chapter structure:** each chapter is a day and a night. The day part is written in the first person, past tense; the night part («the Session») in the third person, present tense, and always begins with the words «Log in».
- **Five parts** are named after the Windows 2000 user groups — the ladder the hero climbs in both worlds: «Guest» → «User» → «Power User» → «Administrator» → «System».

## The main rule of the dream

Each night, more time passes there: first a day, then a month, then a year, then a decade. Igor lives there continuously and doesn't age. So by the middle of the book he is no longer a 25-year-old sysadmin who has dreams, but a hundred-year-old ruler who for a few hours a day returns to someone else's youth — to Vykhino, to a wife whose name he has to remember. This explains his transformation by day: decisiveness, cynicism, coldness.

Every morning he writes in a notebook by the bed who he is, what day it is and what he has promised Sveta. These entries («logs») open the day chapters.

## Part One. «Guest» (chapters 1–6)
October — November 2001. In the dream: the first forty days.

**Our time.** Furniture Paradise Ltd makes wooden windows and panelled doors on the machines of the former Moscow Theatre Furniture Factory (the furniture didn't sell; the windows do well). Igor is new here: the job and the network came to him from his school friend Lyokha, who left for a bank — passwords on sticky notes, the server under the chief accountant's desk. His desk is in the accounts office, among five women who feed him, pity him and don't notice him. The owner calls him «Igoryok», behind his back — the Nerd. His wife Sveta arrives in the first chapter; they live in Vykhino, in a landlady's flat, with surprise inspections. Fixing a mailbox, Igor reads someone else's email for the first time — by accident. Then not by accident. He learns the real cash-in-envelope salaries and starts a notebook of the whole firm's passwords. *On the radio in the accounts office a scientist talks about nuclear winter: if the cities burn, the soot will block out the sun, and there will be no summer for several years running.*

**Session.** He wakes on a riverbank in a T-shirt from the «Komtek-2001» exhibition. He is picked up by people speaking a strange, seemingly ancient Russian. *On the far bank stands a wolf with fangs below its chin — a beast that has no place in the ninth century or any other.* Igor decides: the past, Slavs. Here he is the most useless of all — he can neither make fire nor hold an axe. When they ask his name he says «Logínov» — and «login» is a sacred word to them, «the way in». The priest Khran declares him a Guest: come in, but not let in. Mal, the son of the hunter Bor, is dying; by day Igor searches the internet for the first time not for drivers but for «how to make soap», and the boy lives. *He sees the sea for the first time — grey, with no far shore — and cannot find it on any map.* To stay, he must say the Password; Igor guesses it from the firm's password notebook: «qwerty».

**Rhyme:** in both worlds he is a guest — without rights, there on sufferance.

## Part Two. «User» (chapters 7–14)
December 2001 — August 2002. In the dream: the first ten years.

**Our time.** Igor begins to use what he knows. He gets a raise because he knows on which day the boss is in a good mood. He finds the second set of books, lives through a tax inspection, makes a copy of the disk. Stas, the boor from sales, is brought down by an email «accidentally» forwarded. The boss calls him in to set up his home computer — it holds access to a foreign account. Igor saves Lyusya from the accounts office, unselfishly — for the first and last time. Lunch breaks go on the library: fortification, epidemiology, metallurgy. Sveta notices he has changed; on holiday in Kirov his parents don't recognise him. *In the background, the news: at the office party people say India and Pakistan are massing troops and both have the bomb; the boss, a former army builder, after his second glass talks about missile silos: «It's built so that if there's nobody left up top — it does it itself. Retaliates. Guaranteed.» On television Bush and Putin cut their warheads; in Kirov his father watches a report on a nuclear power station for Iran.*

**Session.** A raid by the neighbours — the Lyubers. Igor organises the defence, and for the duration of the war he is given sole power — «while the smoke stands». He is killed in battle; he wakes in Vykhino, and the next night walks back in alive. For the tribe it is a resurrection, and a cult begins. He marries Yasya, the healer's daughter, introduces a common store, accounting and writing, and stops a plague. Zhila, who took from the common store, loses his rights — «like a user». Igor understands that this is not the past: not a single familiar point of history, *but animals that never existed, and a sea where there cannot be one.* Conclusion: a parallel world.

**Rhyme:** in both worlds he uses knowledge as power for the first time — and he likes it.

## Part Three. «Power User» (chapters 15–18)
February — July 2003. In the dream: the tenth to the forty-fourth winter.

**Our time.** The boss, Ryabokon, is preparing to buy control of the large Meridian Holding: the money is piling up offshore, and the whole preparation of the deal is in his email. Igor reads it like a textbook — and quietly swaps the buyer: at the last step the stake goes not to Ryabokon but to a Cypriot company Igor has bought, Greenlog Trading Limited, with a nominee director. The money had been borrowed from a criminal partner. On the eve of the denouement the boss hugs Igor: «You're the only one.» When the loss comes to light, Ryabokon is killed at the gate of his own house. Nobody suspects Igor. The firm falls apart; Igor wipes all the email. *In the library, where he borrows books on grain and the taxes of Ancient Egypt, a newspaper lies on the librarian's desk: North Korea is withdrawing from the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty. The paper is used to stand a pot of violets on.*

**Session.** Building a state through a sysadmin's eyes: the count, birchbarks, runners, a seal instead of a signature. Access to words — «by clearance». Old Khran, who demanded that everything be put back as it was, is executed as the culprit of a crop failure. Igor orders copies made of all the birchbarks — and reads them. Bor, who remembers him helpless, is sent to a far-off post, where he dies. Yasya grows old; he is still twenty-five. She tells him what nobody else ever will, makes him give two promises, and dies.

**Rhyme:** in both worlds he commits his first real betrayal — and both times finds an administrative explanation for it.

## Part Four. «Administrator» (chapters 19–23)
July 2003 — February 2004. In the dream: more than half a century.

**Our time.** Igor takes a job as a rank-and-file support engineer in his own holding. The HR manager rates the owner's «potential» as «average». He fixes the top managers' printers and reads their panicky emails about the mysterious new owner. His wealth is secret: he and Sveta still live in Vykhino. He begins an affair with Veronika from reception; Sveta finds out. The head of security, Zotov, a retired colonel, starts watching him. Igor uncovers CEO Korneyev's schemes — front suppliers and a twelve-percent overcharge — and calls a shareholders' meeting. *He reads a stranger's blog about 1995: a Norwegian weather rocket was taken for an American warhead, and for the first time Yeltsin was brought the nuclear briefcase open. The comments argue until morning; the last two: «He didn't press it, though.» — «Yet.» On television, with the sound off, they show the Pakistani physicist who sold bomb blueprints to Iran, Libya and North Korea.*

**Session.** Iron, a stone city, a guard. Undesirables are no longer executed but «disconnected». Igor's son Svet grows old before his eyes and dies. A girl from the eastern trail, a relative of Yasya and like her, tells people the Admin is not a god; Igor promised Yasya not to touch her — and keeps his word to the letter: on the square, every tenth person around her is punished. During construction «lower passages» are opened — a tunnel lined with small white tiles and the letters «VYKHI…». He recognises the station. This is neither the past nor a parallel world: it is Moscow in the far future. *Over the metro map he finally puts three years of scraps — the radio, the drunken boss, the stranger's blog — into a single story: there was a missile nobody wanted to launch; there was an answer nobody had time to cancel; there was a winter of many years; the earth shifted, burned, then cooled; the ice came, then went, and the water rose where the city had stood. Above the water only Vykhino remained.* He orders the pit filled in and tells no one.

**Rhyme:** in both worlds he is the secret owner who enjoys walking about unrecognised.

## Part Five. «System» (chapters 24–26)
March — May 2004. In the dream: centuries.

**Our time.** The shareholders' meeting is waiting for the Cypriot owner's representative. In walks the sysadmin — to fix the projector. «I've fixed the projector. Now, to the agenda.» Korneyev is removed; Igor becomes managing director. *Zotov hands him the keys: he keeps the second set — «like at a launch site: two keys with two people, so that one can't do anything alone».* Igor keeps his second promise to Yasya: he tells Sveta everything, from the river and the wolf onwards. Sveta leaves. He moves into an empty flat by the glass tower. *In a newspaper on the managing director's desk — an article about a lieutenant colonel who in 1983 was on duty at a screen outside Moscow: the screen showed missiles flying in from America, and by the instructions he was to report upwards, «and after that it would have gone on by itself». He decided it was a malfunction — and it was. Igor reads it twice and wonders what he would have done.* A new sysadmin, Dima, is introduced to him; Igor types his password shielding the keyboard with his palm — and notices how the boy watches.

**Session.** The empire has grown into offices and a second, secret book of accounts. He is no longer called by name — only «He». In the counting house, between the treasure chest and a tub with a little tree, He notices a junior scribe who carries the letters and knows everything about everyone. He passes on the rule with which it all began — «Read — go ahead. Write — never» — and does not touch him. The circle is closed: every system has its own administrator.

**The ending** is open: Igor lies down — or gets up — and the last words of the book are: «Log in».

| «Igoryok» → «Mr L.» → managing director | Guest → Login → Admin → He |
| The second set of books | The second, secret book of accounts |
| Ryabokon pays for his theft with his life | Khran, «guilty» of the crop failure, is executed |
| Takes a rank-and-file job in his own company | Walks the city unrecognised |
| Can't explain to Sveta why he is the way he is | Can't explain to Yasya where he came from |
| *News of the bomb — background noise* | *A world grown up after the bomb* |
| The new sysadmin Dima | The junior scribe |
