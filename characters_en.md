# «System Administrator» — character bible

## The hero

### Igor Logínov
- **Age:** 25 at the start, 28 at the end (here); about three hundred years lived (there).
- **Surname:** in his passport Lóginov; he changed it himself to Logínov («from the word „login“», because he's a programmer) and corrects everyone.
- **Health:** weak since childhood — bronchitis, asthma, excused from PE, sanatoriums. Military card, category «V».
- **School:** bullied by his classmate Kostya Zhbanov; unrequitedly in love with his English teacher — for her sake he learned the language, which later led him to computers.
- **Moscow:** arrived in autumn 2001 to take over his school friend Lyokha's job. His residence registration was bought from the warden of a hostel at an institute outside Moscow.
- **Origins:** Kirov (before the revolution — Vyatka). Father — a foreman at the timber works; mother — a teacher. Dropped out of the polytechnic in his fourth year to earn money in Moscow. No degree, and he's ashamed of it.
- **Appearance:** thin, stooped, glasses, a bobbled jumper, hands in pockets. Nobody remembers him — that is his main professional asset.
- **Speech:** quiet, full of «probably» and «in principle». Explains in too much detail and gets interrupted. By the end he speaks briefly and never repeats himself.
- **Mind:** systemic. He sees not people but connections: who writes to whom, who depends on whom, where the bottleneck is.
- **Weakness:** curiosity without compassion. Other people's correspondence is first a soap opera for him, then a tool.
- **Self-justification:** «I don't break anything. I just look at how things work.»
- **Habits:** tea in a mug with an internet provider's logo; eats whatever the accountants bring; the morning notebook of «logs».

**Arc by part**

| Part | By day | By night |
|---|---|---|
| Guest | An invisible man everyone pities | A useless stranger |
| User | Learns the price of information | Saviour, war leader |
| Power User | A thief with a clear conscience | Builder of a state; the first executions |
| Administrator | Secret owner; lies at home | A bored god |
| System | Managing director | A function without a face |

**What changes physically:** posture, looking people in the eye, a habit of waiting until others fall silent. Calluses and a tan he has no way of getting. He stops drinking office tea from other people's hands.

## Our time: home

### Svetlána (Svéta) Logínova, wife
26, his classmate, also from Kirov. She finished her degree — unlike her husband — and never reproaches him for it. Sells phones in a phone shop, dreams of a travel agency. Practical, quick to laugh, counts money to the rouble, keeps a notebook of expenses. At the start it's the two of them against Moscow; a real partnership. She is the first to notice the change: he starts talking to her like a subordinate and looking at her like an acquaintance he hasn't seen for a long time. She wants a child. She leaves not a thief but a stranger: his indifference and his infidelity.

### Lyokha (Alekséi) Zýkin, school friend
His only childhood friend, who shared his desk. Moved to Moscow earlier, worked as the sysadmin at Furniture Paradise, left for a bank and asked the boss to take Igor in his place. Arranged the registration and the flat with the old lady. Cheerful, pushy, a womaniser; the accounts office remembers him fondly. It was he who set up the server so that copies of all the firm's email since 1999 sit in one folder under a shared password. In chapter 3 Igor guesses that Lyokha's «charm» rested on reading that email.

### Kóstya Zhbánov («Zhban»), classmate
Bullied Igor all through school — without malice, out of boredom. Later turns up as an employee of Igor's company.

### Yeléna Víktorovna, English teacher
Arrived in the ninth year, straight from teacher training. Igor's first love, unrequited and unspoken. Married the PE teacher.

### Raísa Fyódorovna, landlady
Nearly seventy. Comes without ringing to check the meters and whether «animals have been brought in». Talks about her tenants in the third person in their presence. A small model of power: she has the key. Comes on the twentieth, payday, for 150 dollars — half Igor's salary («The dust is mine», chapter 5).

**The password notebook (chapter 6).** After a virus brought in by Stas, the boss orders everyone to change their passwords and Igor to write them down «in an exercise book and hide it». Now Igor officially has all forty passwords: the boss — «dasha1985», Eduard Maratovich — «leader1», Stas — «stas007», Tamara Pavlovna — «1234», Katya — «sexy21», Oksana — «after_ny» («after New Year»). Stas's wife Ira already knows about Oksana. Sveta hears Igor talking in his sleep («Mal, stir») and begins to worry.

**The first raise (chapter 7, December 2001).** Igor uses what he has read for the first time: from the email he learns an admin's market rate (from 500), the day the Stroyinvest payment will arrive, and asks the boss for 450 — at 10:06, straight after the bank statement. He gets it from January. On the phone Lyokha admits that he read the email for four years himself, and gives him the rule: «Read — go ahead. Write — never. Find out — and keep quiet.» Sveta notices: «Your back's straight. And you eat fast.»

**The second set of books with Igor (chapter 8, December 2001).** Before a tax inspection the boss tells Tamara Pavlovna to show Igor the «Miscellaneous» folder — the firm's whole real turnover. Igor moves it to the server's second disk and takes the disk home for the weekend. He promises: «There'll be no copies»; whether he makes one is left open until Monday. Inspector Ryabchikova gets three windows and a door for her dacha («book as defects») and imposes a three-thousand-rouble fine. Tamara Pavlovna: «They take as much as you won't miss. So there's some left for next year.»

**The copy (chapter 9).** On the night after his «death», at five in the morning, Igor copies the disk with the second set of books onto his home computer: «An administrator must have a backup.» He tells Tamara Pavlovna there are no copies. In the daytime he realises that what he's afraid of is not falling asleep but not getting back. The accounts office explains his sleeplessness by his wife's arrival — the henpecked theory gives way to a flattering one.

**The office party, 28 December 2001 (chapter 10).** The Vstrecha café, staff with spouses. The accounts office sees Sveta for the first time («Small.» — «Then she's big on the inside»); Sveta immediately understands that Katya is in love with Igor. Stas's wife Ira asks Igor to say one word, «no», about Oksana; he stays silent — «you can forward an email with silence too». Ira leaves after hitting Stas with her handbag. Stas blames Oksana: Igor hadn't calculated that. Shame comes for a minute and goes. Sveta: «You're quiet. Only before, I knew what you were being quiet about.»

**The log notebook and Stas's dismissal (chapter 11, 14 January 2002).** A night now holds a month, and every morning Igor reads the notebook: who he is, what day it is, what he has promised Sveta. Once he nearly calls Sveta Yasya. Stas, thrown out by his wife, blames Oksana and writes to the boss that she mixes up invoices. Igor finds a kickback in Stas's email — three percent from Stroyinvest — and carries out the forgotten point six of Eduard Maratovich's «Information Security Policy»: he puts that email sixth in a stack of «spot checks» for the boss. Stas is fired the same day; Eduard Maratovich gets the bonus. Oksana thanks Igor «for the email» — perhaps she guesses. Igor no longer feels shame: «how simple, one piece of paper».

**The boss's house and the Riga account (chapter 12, 2 March 2002).** Ryabokon lives in Malakhovka: a brick house with a little tower, his wife Galina (quiet, «you're thin»), daughter Dasha, 16, who wants to go to England. Igor sets up his personal laptop with a Riga bank's software: the account holds 640 thousand dollars, «not stolen, undeclared», saved over nine years. The password on a slip of paper and a card of codes lie in the home safe; the boss types the password shielding the keyboard with his palm, then turns the screen towards Igor himself. Only Arkady (a hunting friend and adviser) and now Igor know about the account; Igor will come out once a month for a hundred dollars. The boss is a former major, an army builder; he took the factory over from the previous director, Lev Markovich. He tells Igor: «I'm like a father to you» — the same words he used about Stas. Tolik: «I see it in the mirror, and you see it head-on. Four years to my pension, I want to finish the drive.» Sveta: Igor sleeps ten hours and gives orders in his sleep («By the list»); she wants to take him to a doctor.

**Igor saves Lyusya (chapter 13, April–May 2002).** Eduard Maratovich decides to automate payroll and cut the payroll clerk — Lyusya (Lyudmila Ivanovna Sorokina, son Kostya, 15). Having read the memo, Igor teaches Lyusya the new program in the evenings, gives her a separate login with special rights and writes his first memo: without a trained operator the program is inoperable. Lyusya keeps her job and gets a 50-dollar raise; Eduard Maratovich gets another bonus. It is Igor's only selfless act: «I never did it this way again. It never came up.» Lyusya guesses («How did you know?»). Tamara Pavlovna: «You're a kind boy. But you know too much for a kind boy. Alexei was a chatterbox; he was safe. But you keep quiet.» After the film about the ring Igor tells Sveta: «First it should be useful for a long time.»

**Holiday in Kirov (chapter 14, August 2002).** His father, Sergei Petrovich, a foreman at the works (now three days a week), notices his son's straight back and the calluses on his palms. His mother, a teacher of Russian and literature, explains: the surname comes from the church name Lógin (Longinus the Centurion, the soldier at the cross), and the English «log in» is «timber inside». Sveta's sister has a baby; Igor picks it up with a practised, habitual movement, and Sveta sees it: «As if you already had one. And were comparing.» She asks for a child; he puts it off «until the washing machine». At the market Igor meets Zhbanov: a security guard on four thousand, he remembers school as «golden times» and asks to come to Moscow. Igor feels no gloating («a rug isn't supposed to») and writes his number in the notebook. His father: «You said that in a nasty way.»

**The Meridian Holding deal (chapter 15, February 2003).** Arkady is the boss's criminal partner: shaved head, motionless pale eyes, sits in the host's chair; he lends Ryabokon 1.8 million dollars by April («I'm not a bank. I don't have arrears»). The boss has 700 thousand of his own. The seller is Samsonov, one of three founders who have fallen out: 51% of the holding is registered to a Cypriot company, the price 2.4 million (a third of its real value), it needs to be fast and quiet. The lawyer Mark Borisovich works remotely and sets up the buyer's own Cypriot company with a nominee director; the owner's name appears only in a declaration held by the lawyer, and a scanned passport sent by email is enough. All the money is to gather in the Riga account and go out in one payment. Igor sets up a secret mailbox for the boss, «viktor_okna»; the boss uses the same password, «dasha1985», and Igor reads the correspondence from home through a modem. The bank's code card is in the safe in Malakhovka. On the metro the thought comes to him for the first time: «What if.» Sveta goes to a travel-agent course; Igor lets slip that he has been to the sea.

**The theft (chapter 16, March–April 2003).** Igor takes no money from the account — he swaps the buyer. In March, at an office near Kitay-Gorod, for two thousand dollars from the family tea tin, he buys a ready-made offshore company with bearer shares — Greenlog Trading Limited (from «log — a piece of timber»); no passport needed, a nominee director, contact by email with a code word. He hides the certificate on the top shelf. On the evening of 23 April, from the boss's mailbox, he writes to the lawyer that the buyer under the contract will be Greenlog, and asks him not to return to the matter; the lawyer confirms at 23:40, and Igor deletes both emails. On 24 April the boss himself, with Igor dictating, pays Samsonov 2.4 million — and 51% of the holding goes to Igor's company. The boss hugs him, promises to make him head of IT at the holding and «buy him a tie». Igor feels sorry for him «about as much as for Vyun». Sveta finds a newspaper in the tin instead of the money (her bonus for the sea was in it): «I don't need more. I need to know.» Igor notices that he watches her tears without shame. Tolik: «And why are you so calm?»

**Ryabokon's death (chapter 17, May 2003).** On 14 May the boss goes to Meridian as its owner; the CEO Korneyev politely informs him that the owner is Greenlog Trading, which has sent a letter saying «change nothing until further notice». The lawyer shows him the email from the boss's own mailbox; Samsonov is already abroad. On the night of 17 May the boss questions Igor: two people knew the password. Igor answers with the truth alone: the password is the same everywhere, it's written in the password notebook, which has been lying in the accounts office safe for a year and a half. The boss goes through Stas, Eduard Maratovich, Tamara Pavlovna («no, she's been with me since the factory») — he never reaches Igor on the list — and decides he has been cheated by Arkady, who supplied the money, the deadline, the lawyer and the advice about a separate mailbox. Igor stays silent. The boss: «You're the only one who didn't betray me» — and resolves to say it to Arkady's face. On Sunday 18 May he is killed at the gate of his house. Igor is sick in the toilet: «so something still works». The police learn nothing. Tamara Pavlovna (56) asks: «No copies?» — «No.» — «That's what I thought» — and makes a silent pact with him: she knows nothing, he knows nothing, there never was a «Miscellaneous». «I was wrong. Not about „kind“. About „boy“.»

**The end of Furniture Paradise (chapter 18, May–July 2003).** Arkady's people question Tamara Pavlovna («I'm an accountant, I count what I'm given») and Igor. Arkady realises there was «someone else, a third party, clever, quiet, who sat close by», tries this on Igor and lets him go: «Off you go, Nerd. The quiet ones come in different kinds.» He takes the firm and the house in Malakhovka for the debt; Galina and Dasha go to her sister in Tver; there will be no England. Tolik now drives Arkady. Nina Arkadyevna and Katya are fired (Katya goes to Petersburg: «You look — and past»), Oksana stays on half-time, Lyusya gets a payroll job at another firm thanks to the program, Tamara Pavlovna retires to her dacha, Eduard Maratovich stays. Igor resigns, deletes all the email since 1999 and «Miscellaneous», switches off the server, sits in the theatre seat for the first time and carries the ficus home. From Monday he is a rank-and-file sysadmin at Meridian Holding for six hundred dollars; at his interview he was rated «average potential».

**Furniture Paradise's real salaries (file «Pay_real», chapter 5), dollars a month:** Eduard Maratovich — 2000, Tamara Pavlovna — 1800, Stas — 1500, the Dimas — 600 each, Lyusya — 400, Oksana — 350, Igor — 300, Katya — 200. Lyokha got 450 in the same job: Eduard Maratovich called the difference «payroll optimisation», and the boss replied: «The Nerd won't ask.» Igor's first personal grievance.

## Our time: Furniture Paradise Ltd

### Víktor Stepánovich Ryabokón, owner
48. A former army builder. In the nineties he acquired the Moscow Theatre Furniture Factory in the privatisation and opened Furniture Paradise on its machines: the furniture didn't sell; windows and panelled doors do well. Drives a Land Cruiser. Borrows the money for the holding purchase from a criminal partner; when it disappears, he is shot. Calls the computer «the processor» and asks for emails to be printed out. Calls Igor «Igoryok» to his face and the Nerd behind his back. His password is his daughter's name and year of birth. Rude, generous, sentimental; treats Igor like a father. That is exactly why stealing from him is not retribution but betrayal.

### The accounts office (five women, among whom Igor sits)

1. **Tamára Pávlovna Kuzovlyova, chief accountant, 54.** The Soviet school. In her safe the key floppy disk, the company stamp and sandwiches lie side by side. She keeps the second set of books in a squared exercise book. She worked at the factory before; devoted to the boss for many years. She knew everything about the money, but after the theft nobody suspected her — she got away with a fright.
2. **Nína Arkádyevna, deputy, 47.** Forever second. Seedlings on the windowsill, diets, a quiet war with Tamara Pavlovna over signing rights.
3. **Lyúsya (Lyudmíla), payroll, 38.** Divorced, a teenage son. Knows all the gossip, sells cosmetics from a catalogue, feeds Igor. The only person he helps selflessly — for the first and last time.
4. **Oksána, bank and primary paperwork, 29.** A loan on a fridge, an ICQ affair with the married Stas. Her correspondence is Igor's first «soap opera».
5. **Kátya, cashier, 21.** A correspondence student from outside Moscow, an hour and a half on the train. Patience, magazines, English classes. Wants to leave — anywhere. From Noginsk. She has her eye on Igor (chapter 2: «I'd go for him»), and since he doesn't look back, she suspects he's «gay»; smokes with Oksana and Anzhela by the theatre seat.

### The rest
- **Eduárd Marátovich, commercial director, 35.** Talks in phrases from business books: «let's synchronise». Decides nothing.
- **Stas, head of sales, 32.** Loud, charming, a boor. Calls Igor «the computer guy». The first victim of an «accidentally» forwarded email.
- **The two Dímas, managers.** Told apart by their mobile phones.
- **Mikhálych, head of production.** The workshop is outside Moscow. Despises the office, makes good windows.
- **Anzhéla, secretary.** Nails, the fax, knows the boss's schedule and his mistress's.
- **Tólik, driver and dispatcher.** Second only to the sysadmin in how much he knows: he hears everything said in the car. The only one who once looks at Igor with understanding.
- **Nikolái Ivánovich, security guard.** A retired soldier, crosswords, a visitors' log that nobody reads — except Igor.

## Our time: Meridian Holding

### Olég Vitályevich Kornéyev, CEO
42. A hired manager: suit, presentations, the words «efficiency» and «transparency». Steals through kickbacks and front suppliers — neatly, with paperwork. Igor's mirror: also an administrator of someone else's system, only without access to all the passwords.

### Gleb Románovich, IT director
Igor's boss. Understands nothing about technology, understands budgets. Proud of having «built processes». Considers Igor diligent and dim.

### Sergéi Petróvich Zótov, head of security
A retired colonel. The only real threat: he can do what Igor does, but the old way — through people, not wires. He almost works it out.

### Veroníka, PA in the CEO's office
30, a little older than Igor. Free-and-easy, mocking, bored. Carries on an intimate correspondence with a friend and keeps it in password-protected Word files.

**How Igor finds out.** Checking her computer, he comes up against a password prompt. At first he is only intrigued. Then he can't resist and gets into the files. Igor reads everything and starts daydreaming.

**How the affair begins.** He doesn't approach her himself — he doesn't know how. She begins it:
- From the files he is the first to learn she has quarrelled with Kent. That same evening he «happens» to stay late at work.
- She calls him over to her computer on a trivial pretext — she's bored, and the quiet boy seems an amusing toy for an evening.
- From her letters he knows she can't stand talkers and likes it when people keep quiet and look. He keeps quiet and looks: it comes naturally to him.
- She is hooked by the mismatch: the «nerd» doesn't blush or fuss. She doesn't know that in front of her is a man who has lived a hundred years in a dream and had power over everyone there.
- She is sure she seduced him. He knows every line of hers in advance.

His ugliest victory. A reserve move for the future: one day she may realise how he knew everything.

### Álla, HR manager
Tests, questionnaires, «areas for growth». Interviews the company's own owner and rates his potential «average».

### Off stage
- **The lawyer Mark** — a Moscow intermediary who knows his client only by email.
- **Mr Christodoúlou** — the nominee director of the Cypriot company; signs everything.

## Sessions: the dream world

### Yásya (Yásna)
The healer's daughter. About twenty at the start. Mocking, observant, not afraid of him either as a stranger or as a god. The only one who calls him Igor to the end. She teaches him to live there — he teaches her to read. She grows old before his eyes: in a few of his weeks — her whole life. Before she dies she tells him what nobody else will ever tell him. Her absence is the cause of the second half of the book.

### Old Véda, the healer
Yasya's mother. First a rival (he takes her patients away), then an ally. Keeps the old words and doesn't believe he's a god: «Gods don't ask where the privy is.»

### Old Khran, the priest
Keeper of the sacred words — Login, Password, Net. The first to declare Igor sent, counting on controlling him. Loses to his pupil: executed as the «culprit» of a crop failure.

### Bor, hunter
His first friend and war leader. Simple, loyal, brave. Remembers Igor helpless and ridiculous — and so becomes dangerous once the cult is born. Removed quietly.

### Rúdyi, chief of the Lyubers
The enemy of the first war. Clever and cruel. After his defeat — a prisoner and a conversation partner; the only one who talks to Igor about power as an equal. Igor learns more from him than from books.

### Mal the counter and his descendants
Bor's son. In chapter 3 he is six and dying of a festering wound — the first patient Igor tries to save. Later — the first literate official, founder of a dynasty of managers. Each generation of this family serves the Administrator more zealously and lies to him more smoothly.

### Yasya the younger
Yasya's great-grandniece, with her face. Leads the discontented. Isn't afraid of him — like her great-grandmother's sister. He can neither execute her nor let her go.

### The junior scribe
Appears on the last pages. Quiet, unnoticed, carries the letters. He has no name yet.

## Characters in chapters 19–26 (briefly)

- **Igor.** From July 2003 — a rank-and-file sysadmin in his own holding (rating 4.8, «average potential»). Sleeps in the server room in the daytime, takes sleeping pills. On 15 March 2004 he sits down in the chair at the head of the table: «I've fixed the projector. Now, to the agenda.» Managing director. In the finale he types his password shielding the keyboard with his palm, and says to the new admin the late boss's words: «You and me.»
- **Sveta.** Finds the message «Thanks for the lilac». Hears him out: «I believe you were there. I don't believe that you're here.» Leaves on 21 March with two bags and a blanket; sends a postcard from the sea.
- **Veronika.** Invites him for coffee herself; from the lilac she guesses he has read her letters: «You did everything right. A living person makes mistakes.» Tells Zotov; resigns after the meeting.
- **Zotov.** Works Igor out from the Furniture Paradise guard's log (on 23 April he left at five). At the meeting he refuses Korneyev's order: «I serve the building. That's the charter.»
- **Korneyev.** Steals 900 thousand a year through three suppliers. Removed by the meeting.
- **Mark Borisovich.** The same lawyer; represents Greenlog, recognises his client and «does not return to the matter».
- **Zhbanov.** A loader at the warehouse, carries chairs into the meeting («Four-eyes, you're the foreigner?»), then a driver: «Igor Sergeyich».
- **Gleb Romanovich, Alla** — stay. **Lyusya** and **Oksana** are taken on at the holding. **Tamara Pavlovna** refuses: «You're already in a place people don't leave by themselves.»
- **Raisa Fyodorovna.** «When you moved in you were different. But you looked at people like a human being.»
- **Dima.** The new admin, 22, from Ryazan; put in the accounts office between the safe and a palm.
